# Mirror Copywriting Guide & Tone Standards

Read this when drafting or reviewing copy for the Mirror screen, run summary, or share cards.

## Tone Principles
1. **Neutral & Reflective**: Act as a clean mirror reflecting behavioral data without judgment.
2. **Action-Focused**: Frame insights around specific choices made during the run, not permanent character traits.
3. **Bilingual Parity**: Ensure identical emotional weight in both Vietnamese and English.

## Good vs Bad Examples

| Context | Forbidden / Bad | Approved / Good |
|---|---|---|
| Early extraction | "You acted cowardly and fled too soon." | "You chose to bank your gold while oil remained above 60%." |
| Death after skipped exit | "Your greed blinded you and killed you." | "You decided to bypass the exit at floor 6 to seek deeper loot." |
| High Dread push | "Irrational risk-taker." | "You descended deeper while Dread was at 85." |
| Sneaking frequently | "Paranoid playstyle." | "You spent 70% of steps moving cautiously in sneak mode." |

## Starter Banned Words
The following words must never appear in Mirror copy (enforced by tests):
`greedy`, `coward`, `crazy`, `insane`, `neurotic`, `stupid`, `pathology`, `disorder`, `ngu ngốc`, `hèn nhát`, `bệnh hoạn`, `tham lam`, `điên rồ`.
